    Rules
    • Pro-rata – share = your eligible balance ÷ total eligible balance.
    • Days held = multiplier – grows every single day: 0 days ×0, day 1 ×0.29, day 4 ×1.14, day 7 ×2. Seven days is the cap.
    • Share – your balance × your multiplier ÷ the sum of everyone's. A 7-day holder earns 7× what a 1-day holder earns per token.
    • Paid in USDC – every Monday, directly to your wallet, no claim needed.
    • Principal stays – only vault yield is distributed; the treasury keeps compounding.
    • Team earns with you – 1% of each epoch's yield, taken after the snapshot. 0% of trade fees, 0% of supply.
    • Auto-rotation – on each snapshot the treasury moves to the best vault that passes policy.

    A Count the days

    Your payout is your balance × a multiplier that depends only on how many days you have held without selling. Nothing else to track.

    B Every day counts

    0 days → nothing. Each day adds to the multiplier: day 1 ×0.29, day 4 ×1.14, day 7 → ×2. That is the maximum. One full epoch held = full weight, nothing to chase beyond it.

    C Free exit

    No lock, no claim. Sell any amount and the day counter restarts from 0 for the part you sold; the part you keep holds its days. Buying more starts a new counter for the new coins.
